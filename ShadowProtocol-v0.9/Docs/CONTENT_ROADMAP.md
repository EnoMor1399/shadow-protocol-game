# Content and Production Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Prototype
- Embassy greybox only.
- Directorate Nine vs Helix visual placeholders.
- Assault rifle, SMG, shotgun, marksman rifle.
- movement, stance, sprint/crouch, fire/reload, damage/injury.
- one communications terminal, one true objective, one extraction.
- simple lobby/session flow and minimal HUD.

## Phase 2 — Vertical Slice
- polished Embassy art pass.
- 5v5 Protocol dedicated server.
- Breacher/Recon/Tech/Support/Marksman.
- breach charges and curated wall/door destruction.
- camera network and recon drone.
- multiple candidate objective sites and intelligence clues.
- spectator mode and operation review event capture.
- professional audio pass and material-dependent footsteps.

## Phase 3 — Alpha
- add Iron Port and Blacksite 17.
- 15–20 fictional weapons.
- full loadout and cosmetic customization.
- account level, role proficiency, weapon mastery, faction reputation.
- matchmaking and player profiles.
- Task Forces and basic trust/anti-cheat pipeline.

## Phase 4 — Closed Beta
- Old Quarter, Summit and Haven.
- ranked Protocol.
- replay viewer and creator tools.
- battle pass framework and social systems.
- regional server routing.
- telemetry-led balancing.

## Phase 5 — Launch
- 6–8 polished maps.
- Protocol, Team Deathmatch, Co-op Operations, Custom Match, Ranked Protocol.
- full commerce, moderation, trust, live-ops and observability hardening.

## Post-launch
- Blacksite extraction mode (3-player squads, 6–10 squads plus AI).
- campaign expansion and franchise products.
